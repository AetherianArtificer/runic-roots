## Presentation

- Runic's 18 particles keep their sprites and behaviour as data particles.
- Signature effects keep each family's shape language with shaped particle bursts and screen cues.
- Wings keep Runic's models, textures and angles; flapping is a keyframe clip while climbing.
- Runic's client-only cues (chest gleam, scent trail, HUD state runes) are not carried over.
